The studio

Three lanes. One forge.

Music funds the rest. Software ships now. Games and research build the long game. Every lane feeds the others.

01 · Lane one

Music

Heavy, honest, and finished.

WPAI makes heavy music — metal, industrial, experimental, and country crossover under one brand. Every track is written, produced, and mixed in-house. AI handles drafts and arrangement experiments; the final sound is directed and signed off by a human ear. Nothing ships that doesn't hit.

The music lane is the oldest part of the studio and the financial foundation. Flagship thesis track: Weaponized Mind. Releases go on Bandcamp and streaming. Licensing is open to sync and placement inquiries. The catalog grows slowly because quality gates are real.

02 · Lane two

Software

Tools I built for myself. Now on sale.

Every tool in the software lane started as something I needed and couldn't find. RepoForge Pro bootstraps Python projects the right way. The Mixin Field Manual documents what actually happens when 300 mods share a method. HellForge is the in-house studio terminal. Both products are finished, documented, and on the storefront.

The bar is: does it solve a real problem, is it done, and is the documentation honest? If yes, it ships. Free tiers exist because people should evaluate before they pay.

Games & Research

Games and research are the long game. Minecraft modding work (Valkyrien Skies × Immersive Portals compatibility) is already shipped and documented. Original game projects and AI research are in active development — building audience and IP for the next phase of the studio.

How the studio works

One person. Three lanes. Everything ships.

WPAI is a one-person studio. Every decision — creative direction, quality gate, release timing, pricing — is made by one person with full context and no committee. The neurodivergence is the engine: hyperfocus on one lane at a time, pattern recognition across all three, and an allergy to shipping things that aren't done.

01

Build for real problems

Every product starts as something I needed. If I had to solve it, someone else does too.

02

Finish before shipping

Nothing goes on the storefront half-done. The quality gate is real and it's mine.

03

Disclose the AI

AI involvement is visible on every release. The collaboration is open. The human authorship is real.

04

Let the work compound

Music funds software. Software funds research. Research feeds everything. The lanes aren't separate — they're one system.
